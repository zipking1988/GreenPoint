Measured agricultural recognition

After calibration on 20 images, a pretrained agricultural detector reached 96.7% weed precision and 60.7% recall on 40 further public dataset images. The smallest-target recall regressed. These are image-disjoint samples within the same public sugar-beet dataset, with no new model training by GreenPoint. Inspect all methods and failure cases.

Source-image coordinates; GPS, moving-plant identity and physical treatment remain unvalidated or unconnected. The agricultural figures do not replace the published online Meta baseline.
